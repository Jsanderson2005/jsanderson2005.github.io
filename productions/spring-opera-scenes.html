My role

Production Electrician

I designed and built custom PCBs to fit into the internal terminal blocks of 2 chandeleirs to make both of them wirelessly dimmed.
Running off of an ESP32-C3 processor, they connected to a hidden wifi network and recieved unicast sACN.

Production Planning

  1. The original concept showed 3 different chandeleir designs that had to be moved around during the different opera acts
    Due to the requirements of the show, I designed these chandeleirs to be completely wireless using ESP microcontrollers.
    I designed PCBs to fit inside the termial blocks of the chandeleirs to dim individual stems/channels.
    Below you can see the schematic and footprints I sent off to the manufacturer.

    PCB Layout PCB Schematic

  2. This inspired me to make a 'all in one' solution to other wireless practicals considering the limitations of existing systems.
    This would be modular so that you could have different boards, layered on top of each other, to power different LED types: PWM, pixel, etc. With the PWM board having a MOSFET for cutting power when the pixels aren't it.
    Another feature I started to design was a custom USB-C potential difference negotiation board which was customisable through software.
    A lot of this is still in the design phase, which is shown below.

    A snippet of the new PCB schematic

  3. The show was hosted in a studio-type enviroment, with a versatile pipe strucutre above a tension wire grid.
    Due to having such a versatile grid, I decided to model the venue in VWX to make sure that no department clashed with each other
    It also meant that the lighting designer could pre-visualise the show in a more accurate 3D enviroment. The venue was modelled from architectural drawings and textured in VWX. Below is a timeline of the different scenes of the show.

    3D Timeline

  4. This is only a small rundown of this project. There was more I learnt from this project which isn't shown in these files. Feel free to contact me if you want to know more.
